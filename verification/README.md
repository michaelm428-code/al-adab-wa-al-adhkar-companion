# Verification

This directory defines how research moves from a lead to publication-ready evidence.

## Status definitions

### VERIFIED

The original source was inspected, and the relevant text, attribution, and locator were checked against it.

### PARTIALLY VERIFIED

A reliable secondary source, gateway, or attribution was found, but the original source has not yet been inspected sufficiently to confirm the claim.

### UNVERIFIED

An attribution or claim was found but has not been confirmed, or the evidence is presently too weak to rely on.

## Core rule

Verification status belongs to individual evidence items, not merely to an entire hadith entry.

A hadith entry may therefore contain a VERIFIED hadith text, a VERIFIED grading, PARTIALLY VERIFIED commentary notes, and no VERIFIED benefits or applications.

Only VERIFIED material should normally enter the final companion.
